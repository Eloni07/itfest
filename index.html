THE FEST AWAITS

IT Fest 2026 is Don Bosco College's flagship intra-college IT festival — a three-day celebration of technology, creativity, and innovation. Organized by the Department of Computer Science & Information Technology, this event brings together the brightest minds on campus to compete, collaborate, and push the boundaries of what's possible.
